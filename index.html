Our story

The hope in every plate.

Asha's Kitchen began with recipes carried from home: the biryani of Hyderabad, the gongura of Andhra, the filter coffee of Madras. For years our family has helped Evergreen celebrate through Asha Party Rentals, and bringing people together has always been at the heart of what we do. Now we're opening a new chapter: authentic, homestyle Indian food at Evergreen Village Square, a place where good food, family and community come together.

आशाAsha means hope in Sanskrit, Hindi and Telugu. It's what she brought with her, and what we serve.
